# Contributing

Backlog Roulette is deliberately small. Contributions are welcome, but the goal is to keep it feeling like a tiny browser toy instead of turning it into a full backlog manager.

## Good fits

- accessibility fixes
- small UI/UX improvements
- bugs around saving, picking, or removing games
- tiny features that make choosing a game easier

## Probably not a fit

- accounts or authentication
- a backend/database
- giant frameworks for a three-file app
- tracking or analytics

## Workflow

1. Open or pick an issue if the change is more than a tiny fix.
2. Make a focused branch.
3. Test the app in a modern browser, including keyboard navigation if your change touches controls.
4. Open a PR explaining what changed and why.

Keep commits understandable. They do not need to sound corporate.
